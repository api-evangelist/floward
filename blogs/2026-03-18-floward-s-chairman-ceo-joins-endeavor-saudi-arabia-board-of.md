Floward, the go-to online flowers and gifts delivery destination in MENA and UK, announced that its Chairman and CEO Abdulaziz B. Al Loughani, has joined the Board of Directors of Endeavor Saudi Arabia, further strengthening his role in supporting and shaping the region’s entrepreneurial ecosystem. This appointment builds on a longstanding relationship with Endeavor.
